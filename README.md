# 🔴 The Wartime Report

**Open source. Unbiased. Independent.**

Daily briefings on the 2026 Iran War — military updates, economic impact, and international reactions, documented from Day 1.

🌐 **Live site:** [thewartimereport.github.io](https://thewartimereport.github.io)

## What is this?

The Wartime Report is an open-source, independently produced daily news briefing covering the US-Israel-Iran conflict that began on February 28, 2026.

We aggregate and cross-reference reporting from major international outlets — Reuters, AP, BBC, CNN, Al Jazeera, and more — to provide a single, comprehensive source of truth.

**Everything is public. Every source is cited. Every report is verifiable.**

## Why open source?

Because trust in media is at an all-time low. We believe:

- 🔍 **Transparency builds trust** — you can read our sources, verify our claims, and see our methodology
- 🤝 **Community improves quality** — open an issue if you spot an error, submit a PR to improve coverage
- 🌍 **Information should be free** — especially during wartime, when accurate reporting saves lives
- 📊 **No hidden agenda** — our code, our data, our editorial decisions are all in the open
