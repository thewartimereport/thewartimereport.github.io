Corrections & Editorial Policy

Last updated: March 27, 2026

Our Commitment

The Wartime Report is committed to accuracy. When we get something wrong, we correct it publicly and promptly. We believe transparency about our mistakes builds more trust than pretending we don't make them.

How We Handle Errors

  • Factual errors: Corrected immediately. The original text is updated with a note indicating the correction.
  • Sourcing errors: If a source link is broken or incorrect, we replace it with the correct source.
  • Significant corrections: Listed below with date and explanation.
