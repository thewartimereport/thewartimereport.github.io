Navy Maintenance Crisis Deepens as December Hearings Loom

Reading time will be calculated · By agrimshar · The Wartime Report · Published November 24, 2026

Summary

The Atlantic Fleet carrier gap entered its tenth month with USS Harry S. Truman and USS George H.W. Bush remaining sidelined through mid-2027 by maintenance backlogs. Congressional committees finalized December testimony schedules for Navy leadership, setting the stage for the most intensive oversight of naval readiness since the 2017 McCain and Fitzgerald collisions. As operational tempo increases on deployed carriers, questions mount about the Navy's capacity to sustain global commitments with two fewer strike groups.
