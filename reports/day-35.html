DAY 35 — APRIL 3, 2026 LIVE

World Bypasses Washington as Coalition Forms to Reopen Hormuz

3 min read · By agrimshar · The Wartime Report · Published April 3, 2026 · Last updated: April 3, 2026 at 4:01 AM UTC

Summary

The post-American order took another step forward yesterday. Forty nations convened by the UK agreed that military planners from roughly 30 countries will meet next week to discuss collective defensive capabilities for the Strait of Hormuz — without Washington at the table. The stark absence of major Gulf states like Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and Oman signals how isolated both the US and Iran have become. Markets digested the news with cautious relief: oil held near $111, stocks staged a late rally. But the fundamental crisis remains. Just 25 ships passed through Hormuz in the last 24 hours — down from the normal 150 per day. Trump's April 6 deadline is now three days away.

What to Watch

  • Military planners' summit next week: Can 30 nations coordinate defensive operations without US command? Will they risk confrontation with Iran?
  • April 6 deadline: Trump's extended Hormuz reopening deadline is 3 days away. What happens when it passes?
  • Gulf states' fence-sitting: Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and Oman skipped the UK summit. Are they negotiating with Iran behind the scenes?
  • Iran's toll booth formalization: Reports suggest Iran and Oman are drafting a protocol to "manage" Hormuz passage. Is Tehran cementing permanent control?
  • Economic data: Markets rallied late yesterday, but can the relief hold through Good Friday and the weekend?
