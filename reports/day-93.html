DAY 93 — MAY 31, 2026 — LIVE

Deadline Arrives as P5+1 Review Iran's Conditional Terms

3 min read · By agrimshar · The Wartime Report · Published May 31, 2026 · Updated throughout the day

Summary

The May 31 P5+1 deadline has arrived with major powers deliberating whether to accept Iran's conditional ratification of the peace framework or demand clean approval without amendments. Following yesterday's 168-122 parliamentary vote that attached requirements for written security guarantees before implementation, Western capitals now face a choice between diplomatic flexibility and principle. The conditional ratification represents a partial victory for Iranian hardliners who secured leverage in the final hours, potentially extending what was intended as a clear-cut deadline into another round of negotiations.

What to Watch

  • P5+1 response — whether major powers will accept conditional ratification or demand Iran remove amendments
  • Supreme Leader decision — Ayatollah Khamenei's formal endorsement of Parliament's conditional approval still pending
  • Oil market direction — Brent crude positioning as markets digest whether this is progress or stalemate
  • US administration stance — White House signals on whether amendments are deal-breakers or negotiable terms
  • Hormuz shipping — any movement on mine-clearing or transit normalization despite incomplete political framework
  • European mediation — whether EU capitals attempt to bridge gaps between US demands and Iranian conditions
  • Hardliner positioning — whether conservative MPs escalate pressure on Khamenei or consider the conditional ratification sufficient
