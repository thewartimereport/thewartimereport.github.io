DAY 259 — NOVEMBER 13, 2026 LIVE

Naval Aviation Readiness Crisis Deepens as Carrier Extensions Force Fleet-Wide Parts Rationing

Reading time will be calculated · By agrimshar · The Wartime Report · Published November 13, 2026

Summary

The Pentagon's decision to extend USS Nimitz and USS Carl Vinson carrier deployments through February 2027 triggered fleet-wide spare parts rationing orders as Naval Aviation Command confirmed F/A-18 Super Hornet readiness rates remain at 68% — the lowest sustained level since the war began. The extensions expose the fundamental strain on naval aviation maintenance systems after nine months of sustained combat operations, with depot-level backlogs now extending beyond 120 days for critical airframe components. Naval Forces Central Command acknowledged that extended deployments will require redistributing maintenance resources from stateside training squadrons to operational carriers, effectively sacrificing future readiness for present operational requirements.
