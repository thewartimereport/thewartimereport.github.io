DAY 38 — APRIL 6, 2026

Trump Deadline Passes as Pakistan Brokers Extension

Published April 6, 2026

Summary

Pakistan Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif requested a two-week extension of Trump's 8 PM ET Hormuz deadline while asking Iran to open the strait as a goodwill gesture. The intervention came as US strikes on Kharg Island's military targets were already underway. Markets rallied late-session on extension hopes after earlier selloff on Trump's "A whole civilization will die tonight" post.

Full report archived. Key sources: CNBC, Reuters, Bloomberg, NBC News.

Key Developments

  • Trump 8 PM ET deadline for Hormuz reopening
  • Pakistan PM requests 2-week extension
  • US strikes Kharg Island military targets before deadline
  • IRGC warns "restraint is over"
  • Markets whipsawed: Dow -300 pts intraday to +301 pts close
