DAY 86 — MAY 24, 2026 — LIVE

Ghalibaf Schedules First Procedural Vote as 7-Day Deadline Looms

3 min read · By agrimshar · The Wartime Report · Published May 24, 2026 · Updated throughout the day

Summary

Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf announced that Iran's Majlis will hold a procedural vote on Sunday, May 25, marking the first formal movement on the P5+1 peace framework after a week of parliamentary deadlock. The Sunday vote will not be a final ratification but a procedural step to move the framework out of committee, with the full vote expected later in the week. Moderate MPs cautiously welcomed the announcement while warning that any further delays or hardliner amendments could still collapse the deal entirely with just 7 days remaining until the May 31 diplomatic deadline. Oil markets reacted with cautious optimism, with Brent crude dipping slightly on the news.

What to Watch

  • Sunday procedural vote — whether the framework clears committee and moves to full Majlis consideration, with hardliners potentially forcing amendments that could derail the deal
  • Full vote timing — when Ghalibaf schedules the final ratification vote, with moderates pushing for early this week and hardliners seeking more time to negotiate changes
  • Hardliner demands — whether conservatives will accept the framework as written or continue demanding written sanctions guarantees from P5+1 members
  • P5+1 response — whether major powers signal flexibility on timing or warn that delays past May 31 will void the offer
  • Supreme Leader signals — whether Ayatollah Khamenei intervenes publicly to accelerate the process or allows parliament to set the pace
  • Oil price movement — market reactions to procedural progress, with traders weighing the increased likelihood of a diplomatic breakthrough against remaining uncertainties
  • May 31 deadline countdown — 7 days remaining before P5+1 diplomats suggested the framework would expire or require renegotiation
