DAY 26 — MARCH 25, 2026 🔴 LIVE — UPDATING

Iran Dismisses Trump's Peace Claims as Strikes Kill 12 in Tehran

The Wartime Report · Published March 25, 2026 · Last updated: March 25, 2026 — 7:00 AM UTC

Summary

Day 26 of the conflict saw a stark disconnect between diplomatic rhetoric and battlefield reality. US President Donald Trump continued to claim the US was talking to "the right people" in Iran, but Tehran's military forcefully dismissed any suggestion of negotiations. Meanwhile, US-Israeli strikes hit southern Tehran, killing at least 12 people and wounding 28. Iran and Israel exchanged airstrikes throughout the day, and Iran launched a new wave of missiles at Israeli and Gulf state targets. The Pentagon approved the deployment of more than 1,000 soldiers from the 82nd Airborne Division to the Middle East.

Military Developments

  • Tehran struck: US-Israeli airstrikes hit southern Tehran, killing at least 12 and wounding 28, according to Iranian state media.
  • Mutual airstrikes: Israel and Iran exchanged airstrikes on Wednesday, continuing the pattern of escalatory tit-for-tat strikes.
  • Iran missile wave: Iran fired a new wave of missiles targeting Israel and Gulf states, per NBC News.
  • 82nd Airborne deployed: Trump approved sending 1,000+ troops from the 82nd Airborne Division to the Middle East (NBC News, two sources).
  • Lebanon occupation plan: Israel announced plans to occupy parts of southern Lebanon (Reuters).

Political Developments

  • Trump peace claims rejected: Iran's military dismissed Trump's assertion that the US was negotiating with "the right people" in Iran, characterizing the claim as propaganda.
  • 15-point peace plan: US media reported Washington presented a 15-point plan for ending the war to Iran via Pakistan as intermediary.
  • March 28 deadline looming: Trump's ultimatum for Iran to reopen the Strait of Hormuz is now 3 days away.

Economic Impact

  • Oil price whiplash: Brent crude briefly dipped below $100 to $97.57 on ceasefire hopes before bouncing back above $104 as optimism faded — a swing of nearly 7% in a single session.
  • Brent crude: Trading around $98–$104/barrel, extremely volatile.
  • WTI crude: Approximately $87/barrel.

What to Watch

  • March 28 Hormuz deadline: 3 days remain — will Iran engage with the 15-point plan via Pakistan?
  • 82nd Airborne deployment: Ground troops now approved — signals possible escalation beyond air campaign.
  • Oil volatility: Markets whipsawing 7% on every diplomatic signal — expect more swings.
  • Iran retaliation: Tehran likely to respond to southern Tehran strikes — watch for escalation.
