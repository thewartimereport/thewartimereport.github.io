DAY 26 — MARCH 25, 2026 🔴 LIVE — UPDATING

Iran Dismisses Trump's Peace Claims as Strikes Kill 12 in Tehran

The Wartime Report · Published March 25, 2026 · Last updated: March 25, 2026 — 8:48 AM UTC

Summary

Day 26 brought an escalating gap between rhetoric and reality. President Trump declared the "war has been won" and claimed VP Vance and Secretary Rubio were participating in talks with Iran — but Tehran's military forcefully dismissed any negotiations. On the ground, US-Israeli strikes hit southern Tehran killing at least 12, Iran launched missiles at Israel and Gulf states (with a CNN team witnessing possible cluster munitions), a drone attack sparked a fire at Kuwait's airport, and the Senate rejected a war powers resolution that would have constrained the president's authority. The Pentagon approved deploying 1,000+ troops from the 82nd Airborne to the Middle East.

Military Developments

  • Tehran struck: US-Israeli airstrikes hit southern Tehran, killing at least 12 and wounding 28, according to Iranian state media.
  • Mutual airstrikes: Israel and Iran exchanged airstrikes on Wednesday, continuing the pattern of escalatory tit-for-tat strikes.
  • Iran missile wave: Iran fired a new wave of missiles targeting Israel and Gulf states. A CNN team on the ground witnessed what appeared to be cluster munitions from an Iranian launch.
  • Kuwait airport hit: A drone attack caused a fire at Kuwait's international airport (The Guardian).
  • 82nd Airborne deployed: Trump approved sending 1,000+ troops from the 82nd Airborne Division to the Middle East (NBC News, two sources).
  • Lebanon occupation plan: Israel announced plans to occupy parts of southern Lebanon (Reuters).

Political Developments

  • Trump: "War has been won": Trump declared victory and claimed VP Vance and Secretary Rubio were participating in talks with Iran (CNN).
  • Iran rejects talks: Iran's military forcefully dismissed Trump's claims, promising to continue fighting.
  • Senate rejects war powers: The US Senate voted down a war powers resolution that would have required congressional authorization for continued military action against Iran.
  • 15-point peace plan: Washington reportedly presented a 15-point plan to Iran via Pakistan as intermediary.
  • March 28 deadline looming: Trump's ultimatum for Iran to reopen the Strait of Hormuz is now 3 days away.

Economic Impact

  • Oil price whiplash: Brent crude briefly dipped below $100 to $97.57 on ceasefire hopes before bouncing back above $104 as optimism faded — a swing of nearly 7% in a single session.
  • Brent crude: Trading around $98–$104/barrel, extremely volatile.
  • WTI crude: Approximately $87/barrel.

What to Watch

  • March 28 Hormuz deadline: 3 days remain — will Iran engage with the 15-point plan via Pakistan?
  • 82nd Airborne deployment: Ground troops now approved — signals possible escalation beyond air campaign.
  • Oil volatility: Markets whipsawing 7% on every diplomatic signal — expect more swings.
  • Iran retaliation: Tehran likely to respond to southern Tehran strikes — watch for escalation.
  • Lebanon front: Israeli occupation plans for southern Lebanon could open second ground front.
