DAY 36 — APRIL 4, 2026 LIVE

Two US Aircraft Shot Down in Single Day as Iran Proves Air Defenses Intact

3 min read · By agrimshar · The Wartime Report · Published April 4, 2026 · Last updated: April 4, 2026 at 04:00 UTC

Summary

Friday became the worst day for US aviation losses since the early Iraq War. An F-15E Strike Eagle from RAF Lakenheath was shot down over southwestern Iran — the first confirmed US manned aircraft loss of the war. Both crew ejected, but one weapons-systems officer remains missing as Iranian forces and US rescue teams race to reach him. Two search-and-rescue helicopters were damaged during the recovery operation. Then an A-10 Warthog was shot down over the Persian Gulf during the rescue mission. The simultaneous losses shatter Pentagon claims that Iran's air defenses are "degraded" and prove Tehran can still inflict significant casualties despite 36 days of bombardment. Trump's April 6 deadline for Iran infrastructure strikes is now two days away.
