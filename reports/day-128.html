Congress Advances Contempt Proceedings as White House Defies Subpoena Deadline

3 min read · By agrimshar · The Wartime Report · Published July 5, 2026 · Updated throughout the day

Summary

The constitutional confrontation over war powers escalated as congressional committees advanced contempt proceedings following the White House's refusal to comply with the Senate Foreign Relations Committee's July 4 subpoena deadline. House Armed Services and Foreign Affairs committees fast-tracked joint subpoena authority while Senate leadership scheduled a contempt vote for July 8. Constitutional scholars warned the standoff represents the deepest separation of powers crisis during wartime in a generation. Persian Gulf demining operations remained 10-15% ahead of schedule with full Hormuz reopening projected for mid-July.
