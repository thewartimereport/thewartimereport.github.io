Shipping Recovery Enters Third Week as Court Coalition Advances

Reading time will be updated · By agrimshar · The Wartime Report · Published July 28, 2026 · Updated throughout the day

Summary

Global container freight rates maintained stability for a ninth consecutive day Monday, marking the successful transition into week three of sustained maritime normalization and crossing the critical threshold that economists use to distinguish temporary volatility from structural recovery. Chief Justice Roberts' six-justice coalition continued oral argument preparations with no reported defections, positioning the framework for potential resolution of the constitutional crisis ahead of August 12 hearings.
