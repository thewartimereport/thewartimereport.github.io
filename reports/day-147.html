Justices Begin Private Deliberations on Roberts Framework

3 min read · By agrimshar · The Wartime Report · Published July 24, 2026 · Updated throughout the day

Summary

Supreme Court justices began private deliberations Thursday on Chief Justice Roberts' proposed framework for a narrow ruling on the executive privilege dispute, one day after he circulated the confidential memorandum to the bench. Legal sources described initial exchanges as constructive, with justices from across the ideological spectrum engaging with the framework's core premise of distinguishing tactical military decisions from broader strategic policy questions. Container freight rates remained at pre-war levels for the fourth consecutive day, further cementing the durability of global shipping normalization.
