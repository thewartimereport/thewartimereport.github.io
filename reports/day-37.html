DAY 37 — APRIL 5, 2026 COMPLETED

Race to Find Missing US Pilot as Trump's Monday Deadline Looms

7 min read · By agrimshar · The Wartime Report · Published April 5, 2026 · Last updated: April 5, 2026 at 04:00 UTC

Summary

Friday's double shoot-down of US aircraft — an F-15E Strike Eagle and an A-10 Warthog — became a defining moment of the war. One F-15E crew member remains missing as Iranian forces and US rescue teams race to reach him. Iran is broadcasting rewards for his capture while Trump issued a 48-hour ultimatum for Iran to reopen the Strait of Hormuz by Monday. The strikes on Bushehr nuclear facility killed one person and forced Russia to evacuate 198 workers. With the missing pilot's fate uncertain and Trump's deadline hours away, the war stands at its most dangerous crossroads yet.

Military

  • Missing F-15E crew member — one weapons-systems officer still unaccounted for after Friday's shoot-down. Iranian state media broadcasting pleas for citizens to find him, offering "valuable rewards" for capture. US rescue teams racing against time.
  • Two aircraft lost in single day — F-15E Strike Eagle and A-10 Warthog shot down, marking worst US aviation losses since early Iraq War. Two search-and-rescue Blackhawks also damaged during recovery operations.
  • Iran reveals new air defense system — IRGC credited a previously unknown advanced defense system for downing the F-15E, directly contradicting Pentagon claims that Iranian air defenses are "degraded."
  • Bushehr nuclear plant struck — confirmed hit on the operational nuclear facility killed one person. Russia evacuated 198 Rosatom workers. IAEA voiced "deep concern," warning nuclear sites "must never be attacked."
  • Iran drone strikes Israel-linked vessel in Hormuz — IRGC Navy attacked ship, setting it on fire. Escalates maritime dimension beyond blockade into active targeting.
  • Iranian missiles hit residential areas in central Israel — cluster munitions deployed over Rosh HaAyin. One person injured in Tel Aviv. Multiple rounds of attacks throughout Friday.
  • Israel advances Lebanon invasion — bombed bridges around Tyre, isolating the city. Airstrike damaged Lebanese Italian Hospital, injuring 11. Israel outlined plan to occupy ~10% of Lebanon up to Litani River.
  • 13 US bases "nearly uninhabitable" — CENTCOM forced to disperse troops after Iranian missile strikes reshaped American force posture across the region.
  • Iran rapidly repairing missile bunkers — US intelligence says Tehran reconstituting launch capability faster than expected despite sustained bombardment.
  • Iraq closes Shalamcheh border crossing — after airstrike on Iranian side killed Iraqi citizen. War now directly impacting Iraqi civilians and sovereignty.
  • 55 libraries and 30+ universities damaged — Iran's science minister says US-Israeli strikes systematically hitting educational and cultural infrastructure.

Political

  • Trump's 48-hour Hormuz ultimatum — "all Hell will reign down on them" if Iran doesn't reopen the Strait by Monday. Posted to Truth Social: "Time is running out - 48 hours before all Hell will reign down on them. Glory be to GOD!"
  • Iran rejected US 48-hour ceasefire proposal — Fars news agency reported Tehran formally turned down American ceasefire offer, making Trump's new ultimatum even more volatile.
  • Iran's FM leaves door open for talks — shift from earlier "unwilling to talk" stance. Foreign minister "in principle" left door open for negotiations even as military operations continue.
  • Bolton says Trump in "panic mode" — former NSA John Bolton says the downing of two US aircraft likely panicked the president.
  • Hegseth fires Army's top generals mid-war — Defense Secretary ousted Army Chief of Staff Gen. Randy George and Gen. David Hodne. Officials stunned: "It's insane." Destabilizes military leadership during active combat.
  • Trump briefed on ground troop options — including special forces raid to seize Iran's enriched uranium stockpile and potential seizure of Kharg Island. Contradicts his earlier "I don't care" about uranium comment.
  • Republicans warned of "ugly November" — veteran GOP pollster warns war's economic impact threatens Republican midterm prospects.
  • US arrests Soleimani's niece and grand-niece — Secretary of State Rubio revoked their green cards; ICE detained them. Cited social media commentary and ties to "Iranian terror regime."
  • Polymarket under fire — Rep. Seth Moulton (D-MA) ripped prediction market for betting on downed pilot's fate. Platform removed bet after public outrage.
  • Russia calls Bushehr strike "evil" — Moscow condemns attack on facility it built and operates.
  • US military archbishop says war fails "just war" standard — military's own senior Catholic cleric publicly challenged legal and moral basis for conflict.

Economic

  • Oil prices elevated — Brent crude at ~$109–112/barrel (futures), WTI spiked to $111.29. Physical dated Brent surged above $140 on Thursday — highest since 2008.
  • Australian petrol stations running dry — hundreds of stations out of fuel. Government tells citizens to continue Easter travel despite shortages.
  • "War tax" hitting US consumers — diesel and jet fuel prices rising, stressing business profitability with no relief in sight.
  • Five EU finance ministers call for windfall profit tax — Spain and four other states urging European Commission to impose bloc-wide tax on energy companies as oil and gas prices fuel inflation.
  • Airlines in crisis mode — Asian carriers particularly vulnerable to Hormuz closure, with jet fuel supply chains severely disrupted.

International

  • IAEA "deep concern" — Director General Grossi says nuclear sites "must never be attacked," warns auxiliary buildings may contain vital safety equipment. 4th incident near Bushehr in recent weeks.
  • Rosatom evacuates Bushehr — Russia pulled 198 workers from facility. Moscow Times reports "final evacuation" with only volunteers remaining to keep reactor operational.
  • Iraq caught between factions — leaders face balancing act as war exposes rifts between those hoping for end of Tehran's influence and those loyal to Islamic Republic.
  • Iraqi militia claims 19 attacks in 24 hours — Islamic Resistance in Iraq escalating proxy operations.
  • US warns citizens to leave Lebanon — citing escalating security risks following Israeli strikes on Beirut.

What to Watch

  • Missing F-15E crew member's fate — whether Iran captures or US rescues the weapons-systems officer will dominate next 24 hours and could dramatically escalate conflict.
  • Monday Hormuz deadline — Trump's 48-hour ultimatum expires. What does "all hell" look like?
  • Ground invasion debate — Trump being briefed on special forces options including uranium seizure raid. Is conventional invasion even feasible?
  • Bushehr nuclear fallout — confirmed strike on operational nuclear power plant is massive escalation. Environmental and radiological risks unknown.
  • Iran's missile repair speed — intelligence showing rapid bunker reconstruction challenges Pentagon's "degraded" narrative.
  • Diplomatic dead end — with Iran refusing terms and no back channels working, escalation ladder has fewer off-ramps.
  • Gulf state spillover — Saudi Arabia and Bahrain triggering alerts; conflict no longer contained.
